FAQ

Common questions

Who can join the internships and courses?

Students and fresh graduates from any college. No prior experience is needed for the beginner programs.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Internships include an offer letter, completion certificate (e-copy and hard copy), letter of recommendation and notes.

What does journal paper publication cover?

Topic selection, paper writing, plagiarism check, journal selection and submission support. You can choose it with a project or without one. Acceptance is decided by the journal.

Do you build projects for businesses?

Yes. We build websites, web and mobile apps, AI/ML solutions, data analytics dashboards and custom software.

How do I get started?

Send an enquiry from the Contact page with the service you need, and we will get back to you.
